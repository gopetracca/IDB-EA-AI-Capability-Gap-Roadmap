## Blocking — these four prevent publication

### 1 · EDM Council ADAC is not a publication

It is a workgroup, absent from EDM Council's own Frameworks page. **Cited four times.**

> **Action.** Remove. Substitute CDMC if an open data-controls reference is needed.

### 2 · TOGAF capability-based planning was removed from the current edition

Chapter 28 of TOGAF 9.2 was deliberately dropped in the 10th Edition.

> **Action.** Re-cite as Series Guide **G193** (Capability-Based Planning), or **G211**
> (Business Capabilities, Version 2, April 2022, superseding G189) for business capabilities.
>
> ⚠ **G190 is Information Mapping** — an earlier draft of this note had that number wrong.

### 3 · Non-public sources cannot carry evidence

Gartner (subscription) and unattributed "consultancy transformation patterns" together back
**four capabilities**. Both are grade D.

> **Action.** Replace with an openly available source, or reclassify the capability as
> **Synthesized** under ADR-0010.

### 4 · "Institutional practice" names no document

**Six uses.** It is not a citation.

> **Action.** Each must become a named internal policy or standard with an owner and a date.
> Data Governance, Legal and Internal Audit each own one.

## What remains open

The register now pins edition, date, URL, access and evidence grade. What remains is the **locus**:

- **147** capability-source pairs
- **19** have no locus candidate at all
- The remainder carry an **unverified candidate** — inferred from the source structure, not checked
  against the text
- **11** sources are paywalled or members-only, so pinning them needs a licence holder

A capability whose locus is never pinned is reclassified **Synthesized** rather than left claiming
a source it cannot point into (ADR-0010).
